Care Instructions

A wood stamp rewards a little care — treat it well and the carving will stay sharp for hundreds of impressions.

After every print

  • Wipe the carved face with a damp (not soaked) cloth right after use, before ink dries in the grooves.
  • For fabric or oil-based inks, a small amount of mild soap on the cloth lifts pigment without swelling the wood.
  • Pat dry with a soft cloth and let the block air-dry face-down on a paper towel.

Storage

  • Keep stamps somewhere dry and out of direct sunlight — humidity and heat are what warp wood over time.
  • Store flat or upright with the carved face protected, never face-down under weight.
  • A light rub of food-safe mineral oil every few months keeps the wood from drying out and cracking.

What to avoid

  • Never soak the stamp or run it under hot water — the wood can swell and the carve can lose its edge.
  • Avoid dishwashers, harsh solvents, and abrasive scrubbing on the carved face.
  • Don't leave ink to dry on the block between uses — it builds up and dulls fine detail over time.

Custom Orders

Have a logo, monogram or pattern you'd like carved into wood? Here's how a custom order works.

How it works

  • Share your design — send a logo file, sketch, or reference image along with the size you need.
  • We send a proof — our carvers translate it into a line-carve preview so you can approve it before any wood is cut.
  • We carve by hand — once approved, your block is routed and finished by hand in our studio.
  • Test print & ship — every custom stamp is test-printed for ink release before it's packed and sent to you.

Turnaround & pricing

Custom stamps typically take 5–8 business days to carve after design approval, depending on detail and size. Pricing starts around Rs. 2,800 for a simple logo mark and scales with size and intricacy — we'll confirm an exact quote once we see your design.

Good to know

  • Very fine detail (thin hairlines, small text under 8pt) may need slight simplification to carve and print cleanly — we'll flag this in your proof.
  • Bulk or repeat business orders (packaging runs, retail branding) get a dedicated quote — reach out via the newsletter form or WhatsApp.

Frequently Asked Questions

What wood are the stamps made from?

We use responsibly sourced hardwoods selected for grain stability and carving detail, so the block holds fine lines without splitting.

Can I use these stamps on fabric?

Yes — our Fabric Printing collection is designed specifically for cotton, linen and similar textiles, paired with fabric-safe, washable ink.

How many prints will one stamp last for?

With normal use and the care steps above, a well-kept block comfortably holds detail through hundreds of impressions.

Do you ship outside Pakistan?

Currently we ship nationwide within Pakistan. For international orders, message us directly and we'll advise on shipping options and cost.

What ink should I use?

Any water- or oil-based block-printing ink works well. For fabric, use a fabric-safe pigment ink and heat-set it per the ink's instructions.

Can I request a size other than what's listed?

Yes, most designs can be scaled up or down. Mention your preferred size when placing a custom order and we'll confirm feasibility.
